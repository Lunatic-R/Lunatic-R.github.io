I am a master's student (since 2023) jointly trained by the
College of Ecology and Environment, Yunnan University and the
Community Assembly and Species Coexistence Group at
Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Garden, Chinese Academy of Sciences,
jointly advised by Prof. Zhiming Zhang and Prof. Luxiang Lin.

My research focuses on **subalpine forest plant and animal communities and their
environmental drivers** in the Bitahai area of Shangri-La, southwestern China.
Current work combines vegetation surveys (community classification and functional
differentiation) with large-scale camera-trap monitoring of birds and mammals,
examining how people and livestock shape the diel activity and coexistence of
mammal guilds.

#### Research interests

- Subalpine forest community assembly: association classification, functional differentiation, and environmental drivers
- Camera-trap monitoring of birds and mammals; human disturbance and diel activity patterns
- Multi-trophic mechanisms underlying ungulate coexistence (master's thesis, Pudacuo–Bitahai, Shangri-La)
