How are subalpine forest plant communities around Bitahai Lake (Shangri-La,
southwestern China) organized, and what environmental factors drive their
composition and functional differentiation?

Based on field vegetation surveys, we classified forest associations,
quantified functional differentiation among them, and disentangled the
environmental drivers of community assembly across topographic and edaphic
gradients.
