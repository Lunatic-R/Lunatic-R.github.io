Long-term camera-trap monitoring of birds and mammals in the Bitahai area of
the Shangri-La National Park candidate area (Pudacuo).

Current directions:

- **Data paper** (in preparation, to be submitted to *Biodiversity Science*):
  camera-trap bird and mammal monitoring dataset from the Bitahai area.
- **Diel activity** (in preparation): *Human-induced shifts in diel activity of
  mammal guilds: separate temporal fingerprints of people and livestock in a
  high-elevation national park, southwestern China* — how people and livestock
  leave distinct temporal fingerprints on mammal guild activity.
- **Master's thesis** (in progress): multi-trophic mechanisms by which human
  disturbance and habitat heterogeneity drive ungulate coexistence strategies
  in the Bitahai area of Pudacuo National Park, Shangri-La.
